Common Questions

What Military Families in Okinawa Ask Us

Can military families and SOFA-status households in Okinawa book a 4D ultrasound here?

Yes. Family Tree Oki is built specifically for international couples and military families near base in Okinawa. Sessions are by appointment only, so you have a private space just for your family.

Do I need to speak Japanese, or is English support available?

All sessions are available in English. Our English-speaking, all-female team can walk you through the whole visit, from booking to the scan itself, so no Japanese is required.

Can my husband and other kids come to the appointment?

Yes — we welcome the whole family into the room. Guests have told us they especially look forward to this, since children often aren't allowed in appointments on base.

When is the best time to book a 4D ultrasound?

Most families see the clearest images between weeks 25 and 32. Once your baby is bigger and more settled later in pregnancy, getting a good view of their face can get harder, so booking a little earlier tends to work best.

How is a visit here different from an ultrasound on base?

We keep appointments unhurried and take time to explain what you're seeing on screen. Families tell us that pace, plus being able to bring their whole family into the room, is what they notice most.
